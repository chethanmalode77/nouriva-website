About Us

Our Story

A small founder-led export business from Karnataka, on a mission to share India's heritage

Bringing India's Ancient Superfoods to the World

Nouriva was born from a deep connection to the land and a vision to share Karnataka's agricultural treasures with the world. Based in the Tumkur region, known for its rich soil and ideal climate for millet cultivation, we work directly with local farmers who have perfected their craft over generations.

Our mission is simple yet profound: to bridge the gap between India's traditional farming heritage and modern global markets. We believe that quality, transparency, and sustainability are not just values but commitments that define every grain we export.

From careful sourcing to meticulous quality control, every step of our process is designed to ensure that when you receive Nouriva products, you receive the very best that Karnataka has to offer.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you provide lab test reports?
+

Yes, we provide comprehensive lab test reports including moisture content, nutritional analysis, and food safety parameters. Reports are available for each batch upon request and can be shared before shipment confirmation.

Can you do custom labelling?
+

Absolutely! We offer custom labelling and packaging solutions for bulk orders. Whether you need your brand name, logo, or specific design, we can accommodate your requirements. Minimum quantities apply for custom packaging.

What is your minimum order quantity (MOQ)?
+

For sample orders, our MOQ is 1-5kg. For commercial orders, the minimum is 500kg. We understand that first-time buyers may want to start small, so we're flexible with sample quantities to help build long-term partnerships.

Do you have FSSAI certification?
+

Yes, we are FSSAI certified and registered with APEDA for agricultural exports. All our products comply with Indian food safety regulations and are suitable for international export with proper documentation.
